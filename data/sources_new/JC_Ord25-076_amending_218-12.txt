Ord. 25-076 — City of Jersey City, N.J.
AN ORDINANCE AMENDING CHAPTER 218 (MULTIPLE DWELLINGS) SECTION 12
(PREVENTING ALGORITHMIC RENT-FIXING IN THE RENTAL HOUSING MARKET),
OF THE CODE OF ORDINANCES OF THE CITY OF JERSEY CITY.

WHEREAS, the Municipal Council passed ordinance Ord. 25-057 at the May 21st 2025 Council meeting; and
WHEREAS, the Council wishes to clarify the language to ensure the ordinance is as strong as possible.

Section 2. Unlawful Conduct (as clarified):
1. It is unlawful for any real estate lessor, agent, or subcontractor thereof, to subscribe to, contract with, or otherwise exchange anything of value in return for or otherwise utilize the services of a Service Provider.
2. No service provider shall facilitate an agreement not to compete among real estate lessors with respect to any aspect of residential dwelling units, including rental pricing, fees, or any other rental terms.

Sections 218-13 through 218-18: No changes (exempt dwellings, occupancy, extension, violations/penalties, conflicts with state law, findings).

Legislative history: introduction vote Jun 25 2025 (8-0); final vote Jul 16 2025 (9-0); approved by Mayor Steven M. Fulop Jul 17 2025.
Fact sheet purpose: 'Amending 218-12 section 2 to clarify language' (Councilperson James Solomon).
